Audio Transcript

Narrated by a synthetic voice. There is no music or other sound.

0:00 This is StoryCast. On the first of August, 2021, the Hungaroring hosted the Hungarian Grand Prix.

0:09 Rain fell just before the start, and every car lined up on intermediate tyres. Moments after the start, a collision at the first corner took several cars out, and the race was stopped under a red flag.

0:22 As the track dried, Lewis Hamilton lined up alone on the starting grid. Every other car started from the pit lane, on slick tyres.

0:32 Out of the chaos, Esteban Ocon took the lead, held off the pressure, and won his first Formula 1 race. This is the story of the day everything went wrong.
