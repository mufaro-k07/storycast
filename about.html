About & Access

Every Great Story Deserves to Be Experienced.

StoryCast creates accessible audio, video and written racing stories for curious audiences.

Good stories shouldn't have barriers.

Our Mission

Stories Beyond the Result.

StoryCast explores the moments behind extraordinary sporting events.

Rather than presenting only results and statistics, we use storytelling to explain the decisions, challenges and experiences that make each moment memorable.

Our aim is to create engaging narratives that people can watch, listen to and read.

Why This Race?

A Day Built for Explanation.

The 2021 Hungarian Grand Prix was shaped by changing weather, unexpected collisions and difficult strategy decisions.

It provides an opportunity to explore how a single race can unfold through multiple perspectives and storytelling formats.

Accessibility Features

Designed for Different Ways of Exploring.

People experience digital stories differently. StoryCast is designed to provide meaningful alternatives to visual and audio content, while keeping navigation straightforward.

  • CC

    Captions

    Synchronised captions make spoken narration and important audio information available to visitors who cannot hear the video.

    Available now

  • TXT

    Transcripts

    Written transcripts provide an alternative way to follow audio and video stories, including important narrative details.

    Available now

  • TAB

    Keyboard Access

    Navigation, links and interactive elements can be operated without a mouse.

    Available now

  • AA

    Readable Design

    Clear typography, structured headings, sufficient contrast and responsive layouts support comfortable reading.

    Available now

Diagrams in the story include written text alternatives, and the site respects reduced-motion settings. StoryCast describes its access features without claiming verified WCAG compliance.

The story also links to official Formula 1 clips on YouTube. They are external content: their captions are provided by YouTube and are mostly automatically generated. Each link has a written summary beside it, and our own explainer, captions and transcript cover the whole race.
