## Recording notes

- Record in a quiet room, close to the microphone, with no music.
- Leave about half a second of silence before the first word and after the last.
- `/` marks a short breath pause (about half a second). `//` marks a longer pause between paragraphs (about one second).
- Say "2021" as "twenty twenty-one".
- **Hungaroring:** HUN-ga-ro-ring.
- **Ocon:** oh-KON.
