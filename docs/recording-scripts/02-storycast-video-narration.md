## Recording notes

- **Separate files:** record each scene as its own audio file, named as shown (`scene-1.wav` … `scene-7.wav`). The video is rebuilt so each picture appears when its scene's narration starts, so your pace sets the timing and you don't need to match the times below.
- **Times:** the times show where each scene starts in the current video, as a guide to pacing.
- **Silence:** leave about half a second of silence at the start and end of each file.
- **Pauses:** `/` marks a short breath pause, about half a second.
- **No music:** do not add music or sound effects. The captions and transcripts state that the video has narration only.
- **Pronunciation:**
  - Ocon: oh-KON
  - Pérez: PEH-res
  - Leclerc: luh-CLAIR
  - Bottas: BOT-tas
  - Verstappen: ver-STAH-pen
  - Sainz: SINE-ts
